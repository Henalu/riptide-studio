What counts, what doesn’t, and what’s missing +

Data not yet available means there is no verified figure for the stated period. It does not mean zero. Not applicable means the product has no paid offer in this version.

Use
Completed study sessions in OpenTests; reports from users’ own Jira exports in StepBudget. These are actions, not unique people. Product-specific sources and exclusions appear below.
Purchases
Successfully paid orders completed during the period, including orders later refunded. Excludes test, failed and cancelled payments. One person may make several purchases.
Revenue · EUR
Customer payments received during the period, excluding tax, minus refunds recorded in that period. Before payment fees and operating costs. This is not profit. One-off generation payments are not monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
Coverage and dates
The period is fixed, not a rolling “this month” counter. A source and verification date accompany every published number. Partial or observed samples are labelled; missing projects are never silently counted as zero.

Updates are entered after checking an aggregate report or a documented observation. This page has no live analytics connection and does not collect study material, Jira exports or payment details.

03 / HOW I WORK

Enough to help.
Small enough
to finish.

deBarrosLabs is my personal software lab. I want useful experiments to earn their way into independent products.

  1. 01 / START CLOSE TO THE PROBLEM

    Find the actual friction.

    Understand who runs into it and what they do today. A neat idea alone isn’t enough.

  2. 02 / KEEP THE SCOPE SMALL

    Build the minimum useful thing.

    Make one job easier. Add complexity when the product actually needs it.

  3. 03 / LET THE EVIDENCE IN

    Continue, change, or stop.

    Look at real use and feedback. Keep what helps. Be willing to close what doesn’t.
