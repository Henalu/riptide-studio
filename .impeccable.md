## Design Context

### Users

Primary users are startup founders, operators, and small product teams who need a site, platform, or internal tool shipped quickly.

They are usually evaluating partners under time pressure. They do not need a long agency narrative. They need fast proof of competence, clarity on scope, and confidence that the work will actually ship.

### Brand Personality

Direct, sharp, competent.

The voice should feel:

- concise instead of performative
- confident without bragging
- slightly dry, never corporate
- technical without jargon theater

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark "night ocean" atmosphere with bioluminescent teal accents. Editorial scale, calm tension, and strong typography.

This should not become generic AI cyberpunk. Avoid:

- purple-blue AI gradients
- glassmorphism everywhere
- fake dashboard blocks
- hero metrics
- decorative motion with no narrative role

### Design Principles

1. Proof beats promise.
2. Every line of copy must earn its place.
3. Motion should support mood and clarity, never compete with the message.
4. Bilingual polish is part of product quality, not an extra.
5. Static simplicity is a feature; only add tooling when the payoff is clear.

### Quality Bar

Flagship single-page portfolio.
