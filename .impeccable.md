# Design context

Audience: people evaluating the lab's tools, checking progress or making a commercial enquiry.
Use the deBarrosLabs brand and skill, not the obsolete Riptide direction.
Quiet cream, a wide editorial masthead, numbered product index and a ruled public ledger. Yellow is a small punctuation detail.

Mobile retains every metric with labels. Native disclosures explain sources. Dates are explicit and stable. Unknown figures are written out. Visible focus, skip link, semantic headings, 44px targets, reduced motion. No decorative loops, placeholder charts or fake screenshots.

## Distinction from product interfaces

The lab website is an editorial index and public notebook. Share brand fonts, ink/paper colors and the five-part signature with products, but do not reuse StepBudget’s split hero, yellow upload panel, underlined headline, hard shadows or poster illustrations. Each product needs a composition suited to its own job.
